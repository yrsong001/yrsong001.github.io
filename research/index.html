Research

I develop statistical and machine learning methods for understanding complex biological systems. My work connects single-cell and spatial omics with reproducible computational tools and translational questions.

Methods and software

Reliable cell-type annotation with AI

I build ontology-grounded tools that make single-cell annotation more reproducible, interpretable, and uncertainty-aware. OntoAnno combines multi-resolution clustering, Cell Ontology-guided reasoning, evidence review, and report generation; GPTAnno provides a standalone R workflow.

Cardiovascular genomics

Postnatal heart development

I integrate more than 400,000 single-nucleus RNA-seq profiles with Xenium spatial transcriptomics across postnatal time points to characterize cellular maturation, gene programs, and spatial niches during heart development.

Related bioRxiv article

Single-cell dual omics of cardiac aging

I combine single-cell RNA and chromatin-accessibility measurements in non-cardiomyocytes to study cell-type-specific aging phenotypes, regulatory networks, and intercellular communication.

Data resources

Reproducible resources for systems immunology

I help assemble large, cross-study molecular datasets that make immune-response modeling easier to reproduce and benchmark. HR-VILAGE-3K3M brings together 14,136 profiles from 3,178 subjects across 66 studies for vaccine-response research.
